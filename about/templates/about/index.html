ABOUT US

BiteFinder is a small-scale application created by four computer science students at Florida Southern College. The purpose is to showcase a general understanding of working in a team, programming both the front end and the back end of an application, as well as being able to research on our own and learn a whole framework from the ground up. The owners of BiteFinder are Gianna Smith, Daniel Benson, Aubrey Allen, and Matthew Blankenship. BiteFinder uses the Yelp API and a maps API to showcase locations of restaurants you might like, based on filters. It is a more stylish recreation of what Google Maps provides.
